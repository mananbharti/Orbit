Dictation hotkeyPress the shortcut to start talking, press it again to stop. Your words are transcribed on this computer (nothing leaves it) and shown here first - you then copy them or confirm the window to type them into. Off by default.
Transcription modelVerified exact on one English sample clip with tiny/base/small; broader accuracy is unmeasured. Hindi is still rough at every size. On one limited set of 10 public read-Hindi clips (Oct 2026), Large (Turbo) had a word error rate of 38% vs 53% for Small - that is a word error rate over those clips, not the share of words correct, and one small test set, not a broad quality claim. It needs the 548 MB download and runs slower per clip; your own voice and Hinglish are untested with all models.
